# Contestability evidence scope

The reported OptABA-PC contestability audit contains 250 proved baseline
instances: Cancer, Earthquake, Survey, ER(5), and SF(5), with 50 seeds each.
It hardens every fact released by the frozen optimum and proves the new optimum.
The resulting 742 challenges comprise Cancer (207), Earthquake (4), Survey
(507), ER(5) (0), and SF(5) (24).

The fixed-network instances are shared with the main reconstruction study. The
ER(5) and SF(5) contestability instances use the two-edge-per-node synthetic
configuration retained for density sensitivity. This bounded five-dataset
scope was fixed to make all exact re-solves tractable; it must not be confused
with the one-edge-per-node synthetic setting used as the primary reconstruction
comparison. No eight-node contestability run contributes to the reported 742.

For ASPCR-DAG, the 3,133 forced-fact re-solves are sensitivity probes over its
150 Cancer, Earthquake, and Survey optima. They do not establish that ASPCR-DAG
offers the repair/redress interface proved for OptABA-PC.
