# Contestability evidence scope

The reported OptABA-PC contestability audit contains 250 proved baseline
instances: Cancer, Earthquake, Survey, ER(5), and SF(5), with 50 seeds each.
It hardens every fact released by the frozen optimum and proves the new optimum.
The resulting 742 challenges comprise Cancer (207), Earthquake (4), Survey
(507), ER(5) (0), and SF(5) (24).

The fixed-network instances are shared with the main reconstruction study. The
ER(5) and SF(5) contestability instances use a two-edge-per-node synthetic
configuration. This bounded five-dataset scope was fixed to make all exact
re-solves tractable; it must not be confused with the one-edge-per-node
synthetic setting used as the primary reconstruction comparison. No eight-node
contestation run contributes to the reported 742.

For ASPCR-DAG, the 4,330 forced-fact re-solves are sensitivity probes over its
250 Cancer, Earthquake, Survey, ER(5), and SF(5) optima. They do not establish
that ASPCR-DAG offers the repair/redress interface proved for OptABA-PC.
