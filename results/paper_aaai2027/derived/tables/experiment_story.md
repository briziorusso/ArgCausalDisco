# Experimental story

- **Primary claim: optimal repair improves heuristic repair.** The primary study uses the four fixed bnlearn networks and sparse one-edge-per-node ER/SF graphs. OptABA-PC retains significantly more correct facts on 7/8 datasets (Cancer (5), Survey (6), Asia (8), ER (5), ER (8), SF (5), SF (8)) and has significantly higher fact recall on 7/8. It significantly lowers CPDAG SHD and raises CPDAG F1 on 7/8 and 7/8 datasets, respectively; no dataset significantly favours ABA-PC structurally.
- **Why ABA-PC can have higher Fact F1.** Fact F1 treats a retained correct test as a true positive, a retained wrong test as a false positive, and a released correct test as a false negative. ABA-PC's more aggressive release raises precision but sacrifices recall. OptABA-PC optimises release cost and coherence, not truth-labelled Fact F1, so its recall gain can be offset by retaining more false tests. In the primary results, Survey (6) significantly favours ABA-PC on Fact F1, whereas Asia (8), ER (5), ER (8), SF (5), SF (8) significantly favours OptABA-PC; the remaining 2 differences are not significant.
- **Sparsity matters.** On all 4/4 primary sparse ER/SF datasets (ER (5), ER (8), SF (5), SF (8)), OptABA-PC significantly improves Fact F1 and lowers CPDAG SHD. The recall benefit dominates the precision cost in this regime. The two-edge-per-node results remain in the appendix as a density sensitivity analysis rather than being mixed into the primary table.
- **External baselines are graph-only references.** OptABA-PC significantly beats FGS in both CPDAG SHD and F1 on 2/8 and 1/8 datasets. Corrected ASPCR-DAG is matched to 5 primary datasets; OptABA-PC significantly lowers SHD on 4/5 (Cancer (5), Earthquake (5), ER (5), SF (5)) and raises F1 on 5/5 (Cancer (5), Earthquake (5), Survey (6), ER (5), SF (5)). MPC is especially strong on the sparse synthetic graphs because Majority-PC targets one CPDAG using majority evidence across separating sets, whereas OptABA-PC optimises coherent fact retention and can admit several CPDAGs. MPC is not significantly better than OptABA-PC's best class on ER(5), ER(8), or SF(5), and most synthetic MPC outputs do not satisfy their complete recorded trace; its structural margins should therefore not be turned into a fact-level claim.
- **ASPCR is appendix-only.** Validated corrected ASPCR-DAG results are included on the 5 matched primary datasets for which they are available; no result is reported for Asia (8), ER (8), SF (8). The completed ER(5)/SF(5) artifacts use the matched one-edge-per-node synthetic setting and are included in the primary appendix comparison.
- **Recommended framing.** The paper should claim optimal, contestable CI-fact repair that improves correct-fact retention and heuristic-repair reconstruction, with especially clear gains on sparse graphs. Native discrete FGS is a competitive graph-only reference, while OptABA-PC has strong gains over validated ASPCR-DAG. The paper should not claim uniform graph-reconstruction dominance over MPC or FGS, or equate either method's structural accuracy with repair of the tested CI facts.
